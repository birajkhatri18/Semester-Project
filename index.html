📚 What is Swaply?

Swaply is an online book exchange platform that allows users to post books they no longer need and exchange them with other users. It promotes affordable learning and responsible reuse.

How It Works?

Sign up, upload your book details, browse available books, and request an exchange. Once accepted, users can communicate and complete the swap easily.

🌱 Why Choose Swaply?

Save money, reduce paper waste, encourage sustainable reading habits, and connect with readers who share the same passion for books.
