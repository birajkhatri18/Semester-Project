How to Use Swaply

1️⃣ Create an Account

Sign up and login to access the book exchange features.

2️⃣ Upload Books

Add book details including title, type, and condition.

3️⃣ Browse & Request

Browse books from other users and send exchange requests.

4️⃣ Accept or Reject

Users can manage incoming requests from their dashboard.

5️⃣ Complete Exchange

Once accepted, coordinate and complete the exchange.

6️⃣ Track History

All exchanges are saved for transparency and reference.
