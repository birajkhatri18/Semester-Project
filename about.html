About Swaply

Swaply is a user-to-user Book Exchange System designed to help readers exchange books easily while promoting sustainability and knowledge sharing.

📘 Our Vision

Create a community where books circulate freely instead of being wasted.

🔄 What We Offer

Upload books, send exchange requests, track history, and connect with readers.

🌱 Sustainability

Reduce paper waste and encourage responsible book reuse.

👥 Community

Connect students and readers to support collaborative learning.
